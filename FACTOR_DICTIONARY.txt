V21.4 additions (new research hypotheses, not proven alpha)
residual_momentum20: Sum of last 20 observed log returns after subtracting each stock's prior rolling beta times 0050 log return.
residual_momentum60: Same definition over 60 sessions.
residual_lowvol60: Negative 60-session standard deviation of these prior-beta residuals.
amount_acceleration: log(mean actual traded TWD amount over 20 sessions / mean over 120 sessions).
benchmark_beta120: Diagnostic only. Prior-day rolling 120-session beta, at least 108 paired observations; not a candidate factor.

[V22新增]
ks_double_bottom = 雙重底/W底結構代理：前後低點接近、中間有反彈、目前從近期低點回升。
ks_double_top_risk = 雙重頂/M頭風險代理：前後高點接近、中間有回落、目前自近期高點回落；主要用作剔除風險。
ks_capitulation_reversal = 投降後反轉代理：先有明顯回撤與成交升溫，再要求5日價格止穩回升。
ks_cold_resilience = 低熱度韌性：價格仍有正向表現，但近期成交熱度相對120日沒有過度擴張。
